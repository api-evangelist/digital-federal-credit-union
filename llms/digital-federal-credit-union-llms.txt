# DCU

> A credit union offering personal and business banking, loans, financial education, insurance and investment services.

Digital Federal Credit Union (DCU), a division of First Technology Federal Credit Union (First Tech) is a member-owned and operated financial institution serving over one million members with a wide range of personal and business banking services.

DCU emphasizes financial literacy and provides numerous resources to empower users in their financial journey. As a credit union, DCU's member-centric approach translates to competitive rates and a focus on member benefits compared to traditional banks. 

# Products and Services
DCU provides a comprehensive selection of financial products and services tailored for both individuals and businesses:
- Checking and savings accounts (including Free Checking, HSA options, Primary Savings, and Advantage Savings)
- Certificates and retirement options (featuring Regular, Jumbo, Jump Up CDs, and tax-advantaged IRA plans)
- Consumer loan products (offering auto loans, antique car financing, motorcycle loans, student loans, and personal signature options)
- Home mortgages and equity (covering home purchase loans, mortgage refinancing, and Home Equity Lines of Credit - HELOCs)
- Business services (providing free business checking, specialized commercial real estate loans, equipment financing, and commercial vehicle options)

These offerings are designed to support members across their personal and professional financial lifecycles.

# Digital and In-Person Access
Members can smoothly manage existing accounts, open new ones, or troubleshoot access through multiple service channels:
- Online and mobile banking platforms
- In-person service available across DCU’s local branch footprint
- Nationwide CO-OP Shared Branch network and extensive ATM access
- Self-service digital utilities including text alerts, online balance transfers, check ordering, and electronic statements
- Online diagnostic tools to help troubleshoot banking access issues

# Value Proposition
DCU focuses on functioning as a reliable financial partner with an emphasis on:
- Competitive member lending and deposit rates driven by a non-profit, member-owned credit union model
- Fee-free essential services, such as a complimentary monthly FICO score and free notary services
- Specialized assistance and flexibility programs like Skip-a-Loan-Payment permissions and the Financial Recovery Program

# Community and Employer Partnerships
The credit union actively coordinates corporate partnerships to bring credit union benefits directly to workplaces. These community-minded efforts are heavily sustained through foundational programs such as the DCU Cares initiative and updates from the DCU for Kids program.

# Financial Education

DCU runs a dedicated Financial Education Center designed to offer actionable guidance and deep financial literacy, including:
- Financial planning calculators (interactive planners for tracking auto loan payments, borrowing capacity, savings growth projections, and retirement income goals)
- Articles and strategic guides (breaking down how credit scores impact vehicle purchases, utilizing home equity intelligently, and managing seasonal credit card debt)
- Tools and resources designed to help users manage their finances effectively

# Recognition and Trust
DCU is widely recognized for its technological innovation, member-first service model, digital banking experience, and deep commitment to community well-being.

- Named Best Credit Union in Central Massachusetts by Worcester Business Journal.
- Won Banker & Tradesman Best of 2025 Silver for Best Equity Lending.
- Awarded WalletHub's Best Credit-Builder Loans Overall for 2025.
